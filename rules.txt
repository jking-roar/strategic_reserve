# STRATEGIC RESERVE

### by Mark Steere

## INTRODUCTION

Strategic Reserve is a two-player game of skill and luck played on a **6×6** board. Each player begins with **6 checkers in reserve**. The board starts with twelve checkers arranged as follows:

```
Reserve (Far Player): 6

......
.RBRB.
.B..R.
.R..B.
.BRBR.
......

Reserve (Near Player): 6
```

Legend:

* `R` = Red checker
* `B` = Blue checker
* `.` = Empty square

The arrangement above is the game's starting position. 

---

## RESERVE

Each player begins with **6 checkers** in their reserve (a supply kept off the board). Whenever you place a checker onto the board, it comes from your reserve. 

---

## DICE

Two six-sided dice determine one square on the board.

* Purple die = horizontal coordinate (column)
* Green die = vertical coordinate (row)

Columns are numbered **left to right** from your perspective.

Rows are numbered **near to far** from your perspective. 

---

## PLAY

On your turn:

1. Roll both dice.
2. Resolve any removals caused by the rolled square.
3. Place one checker from your reserve. 

---

## GROUP

A **group** consists of one or more orthogonally connected (up, down, left, right) checkers of the same color.

Diagonal connections do not count.

A group is always maximal—it cannot be a subset of a larger connected group. 

---

# HITS

## 1. Hitting an enemy group

If the rolled square belongs to an enemy group:

* Remove the **entire connected group**.
* Return all removed checkers to your opponent's reserve.
* Place one checker onto **any empty square**.

Example (Figure 2a)

Dice:

```
Purple = 5
Green  = 2
```

Board:

```
RR...R
RRB.RB
BB.R..
...BBR
.B..B.
R..R..
```

Yellow hit:

```
RR...R
RRB.RB
BB.R..
...BYR
.B..B.
R..R..
```

(`Y` marks the rolled square.)

The connected blue group containing `Y` is removed before placing a new checker. 

---

## 2. Hitting a friendly group

If the rolled square belongs to one of your own groups:

* Remove **every adjacent enemy group**.
* Return those groups to your opponent's reserve.
* Place your new checker on an **empty orthogonally adjacent square** to the friendly group.

Example (Figures 2b–2c)

Dice:

```
Purple = 6
Green  = 3
```

Friendly group struck:

```
RR...R
RRB.RB
YB.R..
.B...R
.B....
R..R..
```

(`Y` marks the rolled square.)

After removing adjacent enemy groups, the legal placement squares are:

```
......
xx....
..x...
xx....
......
......
```

where:

* `x` = legal placement location

One of those squares must receive the newly placed checker. 

---

## 3. Hitting nothing

If the rolled square is empty:

* Place one checker onto **any empty square**. 

---

## OBJECT OF THE GAME

The first player to empty their reserve wins.

You win immediately after placing the last checker from your reserve. 

---

## DESIGN NOTES

The designer wanted to create a game in which a dice roll identifies a single square on the board. Strategic Reserve is presented as the solution to that design challenge.
